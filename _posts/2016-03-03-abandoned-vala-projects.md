There was a time when Vala was really popular, and a plethora of Vala apps spawned. A lot of them are dead right now, so since i don't have time to revive any of them, i'll publish this list of interesting projects in case someone is interested in bringing back one of them:

- [Ease (Presentation software)](http://www.ease-project.org/)
- [Lombard (Video Editor) and Fillmore (Audio Editor)](https://github.com/yorba/media)
- [ODG - Display Gauges on GTK+](https://github.com/OldRepoPreservation/odg)
- [Finalterm, an awesome terminal emulator](https://github.com/p-e-w/finalterm)
- ~~[libgtkflow, Flowgraphs on GTK+](https://github.com/grindhold/libgtkflow)~~ \[Updated 2017-04-08: Not dead. Sorry grindhold.\]
- [Books, a simple book manager](https://github.com/diegotoral/Books)
- ~~[World in a Box - Game/Game Maker in Vala](https://github.com/steffen123/wiab)~~ \[Updated 2017-04-08: Gone.\]
- [Valatra, like Ruby's Sinatra, but written in Vala](https://github.com/erik/valatra)
